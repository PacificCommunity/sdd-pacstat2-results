
This folder holds raw data such as lookup tables.

Contents:

## out-of-scope-sdg-series.csv 

A list of selected SDG series (not all of them), marked TRUE where they are out of scope for the PacStat 2 indicator counting exercise because they are not national statisitcs as such, but indicators of legal frameworks or similar, or are statistics but not the country's responsibility e.g. financial flows from donors.
